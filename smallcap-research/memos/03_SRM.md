# SRM Contractors (NSE: SRM)

> Research memo, not investment advice. Figures are from secondary aggregators and company releases found by web search in Nov 2026; anything marked *unverified* has not been checked against filings.

**Composite score 7.95/10** (rank 3 of the finalists) · market cap Rs 988 Cr · Engineering & Construction

## 1. Two-minute drill

SRM is an EPC contractor for roads, tunnels and slope stabilisation, with roots in Jammu & Kashmir and now national work such as a Rs 483 Cr Nashik Ring Road package. FY26 revenue roughly doubled to about Rs 1,026 Cr and profit to Rs 111 Cr, and FY27 guidance is Rs 1,400-1,750 Cr. For that, it must execute the Rs 1,800-3,000 Cr order book on time and get paid by government agencies without a large working-capital build.

## 3. Thinking ladder

1. How does a contractor grow 94% in a year? A small base plus a few large awards.
2. 2nd order: fast EPC growth eats working capital, which is why FY25 CFO was negative. Profits arrive before cash.
3. 3rd order: as it bids for bigger packages it meets larger rivals on price, and tunnelling carries geological cost-overrun risk. Margin normalisation is the base case, not the bear case.

## 4. Customer and product angle

B2B and government: NHIDCL, BRO, MoRTH and state PWDs, so a few large projects dominate. What to check: payment timelines, retention money, arbitration claims, and how the FY26 receivables and contract assets compare with revenue.

*Provisional product score (desk-based, product not tried): 5/10.*

## 5. PE/VC view

Asset-light but working-capital heavy, with a 32% ROCE. Headroom is large given the infrastructure pipeline, but the business is cyclical. The exit path is a re-rating from a P/E of 7.7 and index inclusion.

*Headroom score: 7/10.*

## 6. Red flags

- FY25 CFO was negative (about -Rs 47 Cr on a -Rs 116 Cr working-capital build); FY26 CFO recovered to +Rs 92 Cr (Finology).
- Concentrated promoter holding (72.6%) and a thin free float.
- Fixed-price EPC risk, especially in tunnels.
