**Composite score 7.86/10** (rank 7 of the finalists) · market cap Rs 1,932 Cr · Specialty Industrial Machinery

## 1. Two-minute drill

Krishna Defence supplies special steel and fabricated items for naval shipbuilding. FY26 revenue rose 29% to Rs 245 Cr and profit 86% to Rs 41 Cr, at a 21.3% EBITDA margin. The order book is only Rs 103 Cr against a Rs 221 Cr tender pipeline, so growth depends on winning new shipyard orders (corvettes, next-gen vessels) quickly.

## 3. Thinking ladder

1. Why does a small company supply warship steel? It holds qualified special-steel (DMR-grade) vendor status, which takes years to earn.
2. 2nd order: revenue follows shipyard build schedules, so it is lumpy, and an order book of under half a year's revenue gives little visibility.
3. 3rd order: the indigenisation push brings more vendors and larger steel makers into special grades, so the window of scarcity may narrow just as volumes rise.

## 4. Customer and product angle

B2B. Customers are shipyards (MDL, GRSE, CSL, GSL) and the MoD (e.g. a ~Rs 45.6 Cr special-steel contract). What to check: repeat orders per shipyard, approval status for new grades, and payment days.

*Provisional product score (desk-based, product not tried): 5/10.*

## 5. PE/VC view

Low-capex fabrication with a 26% ROCE. At a P/E of 35.6 the growth is already priced in. The exit path is a strategic buyer among defence majors or index inclusion.

*Headroom score: 6/10.*

## 6. Red flags

- Thin order book: about 0.4x annual revenue.
- Promoter stake drifting down (62.3% -> 59.9%).
- CFO is not verified.
