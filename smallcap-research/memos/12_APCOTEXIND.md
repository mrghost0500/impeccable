# Apcotex Industries (NSE: APCOTEXIND)

> Research memo, not investment advice. Figures are from secondary aggregators and company releases found by web search in Nov 2026; anything marked *unverified* has not been checked against filings.

**Composite score 7.31/10** (rank 12 of the finalists) · market cap Rs 2,509 Cr · Specialty Chemicals

## 1. Two-minute drill

Apcotex makes synthetic latex and rubber (NBR, SBR, VP latex) for paper coating, carpets, tyre cord, gloves and construction. FY26 revenue rose only 3.5% to Rs 1,442 Cr, but profit rose 88% to Rs 101 Cr as margins recovered. It has net cash of about Rs 70 Cr and a Rs 210 Cr expansion under way. For the profit gains to hold, margins must withstand Chinese imports and the new capacity has to fill.

## 3. Thinking ladder

1. A specialty polymer business, so why did profit nearly double on flat revenue? Margins recovered from a trough.
2. 2nd order: Chinese export pricing sets the ceiling on margins.
3. 3rd order: anti-dumping duties on NBR protect domestic producers; if they lapse, the recovery reverses.

## 4. Customer and product angle

B2B. Customers are tyre, glove, paper and carpet makers. What to check: customer concentration, export share, and the status of anti-dumping duties. Nothing for an individual to try.

*Provisional product score (desk-based, product not tried): 5/10.*

## 5. PE/VC view

Capex-heavy with a 28% ROCE. Headroom is moderate and the business is cyclical.

*Headroom score: 4/10.*

- ~Rs 18 Cr of one-off provisions in Q4 FY26.
