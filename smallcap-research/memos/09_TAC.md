# TAC Infosec (NSE: TAC)

**Composite score 7.74/10** (rank 9 of the finalists) · market cap Rs 751 Cr · Software - Application

## 1. Two-minute drill

TAC Infosec sells cybersecurity vulnerability-management and risk-quantification software (its ESOF platform) and is listed on NSE SME. FY26 revenue rose 88% to Rs 57 Cr and profit 78% to Rs 26 Cr, at a 54% EBITDA margin. H1 got a lift from two large partner deals, and H2 profit fell 31%. For the story to work, those deals have to turn into recurring revenue and the global partner channel has to keep producing orders.

## 3. Thinking ladder

1. How does a Rs 57 Cr software company earn 54% EBITDA? A small team, partner-led selling and a product that is mostly built.
2. 2nd order: large partner orders make revenue lumpy, and the H2 slowdown shows it.
3. 3rd order: it competes with Tenable, Qualys and Rapid7, which outspend it on R&D and sales, so its edge has to come from price or the risk-quantification niche.

## 4. Customer and product angle

B2B. The buyers are CISOs at banks and enterprises. What to check: ARR, net revenue retention, partner concentration, and customer references; collection was ~80% of FY26 invoicing.

*Provisional product score (desk-based, product not tried): 5/10.*

## 5. PE/VC view

Asset-light, high-margin and with large headroom. The exit path is SME-to-main-board migration or a strategic buyer among security vendors. A P/E of 33.6 needs sustained growth.

*Headroom score: 7/10.*

## 6. Red flags

- Tiny revenue base with lumpy deals.
- Promoter holding is 56.7% (the feed showed 71.5%); FII holding fell from 3.8% to 0%.
- SME listing.
