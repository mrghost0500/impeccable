## 1. Two-minute drill

KP Green is the KP Group's engineering arm. It fabricates lattice transmission towers, solar module-mounting structures and substation structures. FY26 revenue rose 79% to Rs 1,246 Cr and profit 85% to Rs 136 Cr, with an order book of Rs 1,831 Cr plus Rs 508 Cr won in May 2026. For this to continue, renewable and transmission capex must stay strong and the share of revenue from outside the group has to grow.

## 3. Thinking ladder

1. Why so much growth so fast? The group's own renewable build-out plus national transmission capex.
2. 2nd order: if sister companies (KPI Green, KP Energy) are major customers, related parties are setting its prices and margins, so profit quality depends on arm's-length terms.
3. 3rd order: steel prices and competition from large fabricators (KEC, Skipper and others) cap margins once group demand plateaus.

## 4. Customer and product angle

B2B. Customers are group companies and utilities. What to check: the related-party share of revenue and of receivables in the annual report, and the order wins from non-group customers.

*Provisional product score (desk-based, product not tried): 4/10.*

## 5. PE/VC view

Moderate capex with a 29% ROCE and large headroom. The exit path is SME-to-main-board migration.

*Headroom score: 7/10.*

## 6. Red flags

- D/E of 1.35 in the screener feed.
