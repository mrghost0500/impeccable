## 1. Two-minute drill

Unified Data-Tech is a BSE-SME-listed IT infrastructure integrator and AMC provider for enterprises and banks. FY26 revenue rose about 31% to ~Rs 288 Cr, while profit rose only 19% to ~Rs 41 Cr as margins compressed. It now plans to enter safety equipment. For the case to work, the core deal flow has to continue and the new venture must not dilute returns.

## 3. Thinking ladder

1. Why does a system integrator earn a 35% ROCE? Thin capital needs and recurring AMC income.
2. 2nd order: hardware-resale-heavy revenue faces constant price competition, which shows up as margin compression.
3. 3rd order: moving into unrelated safety equipment suggests the core business has limited reinvestment room. That is a capital-allocation signal.

## 4. Customer and product angle

B2B. The end users are bank and enterprise IT teams. What to check: AMC renewal rates, OEM partner tier status, and the top-5 customer share (e.g. the Rs 4.24 Cr follow-on AMC from a private bank).

*Provisional product score (desk-based, product not tried): 5/10.*

## 5. PE/VC view

Asset-light with moderate headroom. The exit path is SME-to-main-board migration or a sale to a larger integrator.

*Headroom score: 5/10.*

## 6. Red flags

- Unrelated diversification into safety equipment.
- SME listing: thin liquidity and lighter disclosure.
- CFO is not verified.
