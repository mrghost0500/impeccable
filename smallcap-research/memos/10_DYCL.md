# Dynamic Cables (NSE: DYCL)

> Research memo, not investment advice. Figures are from secondary aggregators and company releases found by web search in Nov 2026; anything marked *unverified* has not been checked against filings.

**Composite score 7.72/10** (rank 10 of the finalists) · market cap Rs 1,751 Cr · Electrical Equipment & Parts

## 1. Two-minute drill

Dynamic Cables makes power cables and conductors for discoms, EPC contractors and railways. FY26 revenue rose 17% to Rs 1,198 Cr and profit 30% to Rs 84 Cr, and the order book is Rs 829 Cr (Sep-26). H1 FY27 profit was Rs 47 Cr. For growth to continue, discom capex (RDSS) has to continue, and copper and aluminium costs have to keep passing through to customers.

## 3. Thinking ladder

1. Cables are close to a commodity, so why does Dynamic earn a 26% ROCE? Utility approvals and steady discom tenders.
2. 2nd order: margins depend on metal pass-through clauses and on competition in L1 tenders.
3. 3rd order: the RDSS scheme's 2026 timeline means order inflow could slow just as larger cable makers chase the same tenders. Exports are the hedge.

## 4. Customer and product angle

B2B. Customers are discoms, EPC contractors and railways. What to check: payment days from discoms, customer concentration, and the order book's split by customer type.

*Provisional product score (desk-based, product not tried): 5/10.*

## 5. PE/VC view

Moderate capex, a 26% ROCE and moderate headroom. The exit path is index inclusion.

*Headroom score: 5/10.*

## 6. Red flags

- Q4 FY26 margin pressure (the stock fell about 19% on results).
- Bank-facility ratings were withdrawn at the company's request (check that this was because debt was repaid).
- CFO/PAT about 0.6 on the available data.
