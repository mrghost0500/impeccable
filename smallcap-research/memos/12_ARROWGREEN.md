# Arrow Greentech (NSE: ARROWGREEN)

> Research memo, not investment advice. Figures are from secondary aggregators and company releases found by web search in Nov 2026; anything marked *unverified* has not been checked against filings.

**Composite score 7.63/10** (rank 12 of the finalists) · market cap Rs 1,038 Cr · Packaging & Containers

## 1. Two-minute drill

Arrow makes water-soluble and biodegradable films (its Green segment) and security and anti-counterfeiting products (its High-tech segment), and holds 24 patents. FY26 revenue fell 18% and profit 25% on lumpy security orders, while the Green segment grew 52%. A reported Q1 FY27 rebound (revenue Rs 90 Cr, profit Rs 27 Cr) is unverified. For the case to work, security orders must recover and Green must scale.

## 3. Thinking ladder

1. How does a film maker earn a 30%+ ROCE? Patented niche films with few competitors.
2. 2nd order: the security business depends on government and institutional orders, so earnings swing year to year.
3. 3rd order: plastic bans and extended-producer-responsibility rules could expand demand for biodegradable film, but certification costs and Chinese price competition will decide who captures it.

## 4. Customer and product angle

B2B. Customers are detergent-pod and agrochemical-packaging makers and government/security printers. Try the product by buying a detergent pod or other water-soluble pack, then check who supplies the film and whether those customers reorder.

*Provisional product score (desk-based, product not tried): 5/10.*

## 5. PE/VC view

Asset-light and cash-rich with moderate headroom. Specialty-film majors (Kuraray/MonoSol type) are possible strategic buyers.

*Headroom score: 5/10.*

## 6. Red flags

- Promoter stake down ~3.8pp (68.6% -> 64.8%).
- CFO is not verified.
