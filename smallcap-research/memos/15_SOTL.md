# Savita Oil Technologies (NSE: SOTL)

> Research memo, not investment advice. Figures are from secondary aggregators and company releases found by web search in Nov 2026; anything marked *unverified* has not been checked against filings.

**Composite score 6.60/10** (rank 15 of the finalists) · market cap Rs 4,461 Cr · Specialty Chemicals

## 1. Two-minute drill

Savita makes transformer oils, white oils and lubricants (the Savsol brand). FY26 revenue was a record Rs 4,363 Cr and profit rose 61% to Rs 182 Cr. Q1 FY27 profit of about Rs 292 Cr was a one-quarter windfall. For the case to work, transformer-oil demand has to stay strong with the grid build-out and base-oil spreads have to hold.

## 3. Thinking ladder

1. The transformer-oil leader benefits directly from India's grid and transformer build-out.
2. 2nd order: base-oil price swings create large inventory gains and losses, which is what the Q1 FY27 spike was.
3. 3rd order: the shift to EVs shrinks automotive lubricant demand over time; transformer and industrial oils are the durable part.

## 4. Customer and product angle

Mixed. Transformer OEMs are B2B; Savsol lubricants are sold through mechanics and retail. Try the product: check Savsol's shelf presence and price against Castrol/Gulf at a local auto-parts shop and ask mechanics which brand they recommend.

*Provisional product score (desk-based, product not tried): 5/10.*

## 5. PE/VC view

A mature business with low-to-moderate headroom, already 14% institutionally owned.

*Headroom score: 4/10.*

## 6. Red flags

- The TTM P/E of 7.6 is a windfall artefact; normalised it is ~24.5.
- Pending merger with a promoter entity (Savita GreenTec): check the swap ratio and the related-party terms.
- Crude and base-oil cyclicality.
