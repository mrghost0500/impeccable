> Research memo, not investment advice. Figures are from secondary aggregators and company releases found by web search in Nov 2026; anything marked *unverified* has not been checked against filings.

**Composite score 7.18/10** (rank 14 of the finalists) · market cap Rs 1,134 Cr · Electrical Equipment & Parts

## 1. Two-minute drill

Modison makes silver-based and copper-tungsten electrical contacts for switchgear makers. FY26 revenue rose 45% to Rs 710 Cr and profit 194% to Rs 73 Cr, much of it riding on the silver price (inventory and hedge gains). Q1 FY27 profit of Rs 34 Cr continues that pattern. For the case to work, power transmission and distribution capex must keep switchgear volumes up, and silver must not reverse sharply.

## 3. Thinking ladder

1. A small part inside every breaker: switchgear makers need months to qualify a new contact supplier, so switching costs are high.
2. 2nd order: the current profits are inflated by silver price gains, so earnings need normalising before applying any multiple.
3. 3rd order: if silver falls, the inventory gains reverse; hedging limits this but showed up as exceptional items.

## 4. Customer and product angle

B2B. Customers are switchgear OEMs (ABB/Siemens/Schneider/L&T type). What to check: approval status with each OEM, Modison's share of their contact purchases, and how silver costs pass through.

*Provisional product score (desk-based, product not tried): 6/10.*

## 5. PE/VC view

Working-capital heavy because it holds silver inventory. Headroom comes from power transmission and distribution capex. A global contact maker is a plausible strategic buyer.

*Headroom score: 5/10.*
