Walk into the first light.

Guided dawn treks across the high ridges, timed so the sun comes up while you are standing on top of it.

See departures Scroll the route
  • Groups of eight
  • Leaves 4:40 am
  • Back by 10:00

01 / Valley floor

Torches off at the tree line.

The first hour is by headlamp through pine and frost. Your guide sets a pace you can talk at, and nobody is left at the back.

02 / The ridge

Six ranges, one colour at a time.

The ridge path opens out above the haze. Each range turns from violet to rose as the light climbs behind you.

03 / Summit, 1,920 m

Coffee on the top cairn.

Breakfast is carried up for you. You are back at the lodge before most people are awake.
