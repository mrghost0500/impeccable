## The house (intro)
In 1928 a lemon merchant built this house where the cliff was too steep for anyone else. His crates
went down 214 steps to a boat and on to Naples. His lemons still grow on the terraces, and the boat
still leaves at eight and comes back at seven. At the bottom of the steps is a flat rock where you can
swim before breakfast.

Note: Open 1 April to 25 October. Twelve rooms, two of them for families. Breakfast and the boat are
included.

## Questions (before you come)
Heading: Before you come
Facts (sample): Via del Capo 14, Marina di Vento. Check-in from 14:00, check-out by 11:00. Naples airport
is 75 minutes by car to the gate at the top.

- How do bags get down the steps? Our porters carry them. The road ends at the top gate, and the house
  is 40 steps below it.
- Are the 214 steps the only way to the sea? They are wide, with rails and two benches on the way. If
  you would rather not climb, the boat can take you from the house landing to the beach at Marina Grande.
- Can children stay? Yes, in Ostro and Garbino, which sleep four. The other rooms are for two.
- Is the house accessible? It is built into a cliff. A ramp from the top gate reaches Ostro and the
  lemon terrace without steps; the rest of the house has stairs. Tell us what you need and we will plan
  your stay with you.
- Do I need to book dinner? Yes. The terrace has nine tables.
- What if the sea is rough? Our driver takes you to town by road at the same times as the boat.
